1. The Main Dashboard

When you open Chronos, the main screen displays all of your active countdown cards along with a live count of tracked events under the CHRONOS header.

  • Live 6-Unit Display: Each card updates every second, showing Years (YRS), Months (MOS), Days (DAYS), Hours (HRS), Minutes (MINS), and Seconds (SECS) on a single row.
  • Arrived Events: Once an event's target date passes, the card switches to elapsed time and displays an "Event has arrived! (Counting elapsed time)" banner.
  • Quick Actions: Tap any card to open its full Event Overview, tap the ⋮ button on the top-right of a card for quick actions (Move Up, Move Down, Edit, Delete), or tap + New Event at the bottom right to create a countdown.

2. Creating & Editing Countdowns

  1. Tap the + New Event button in the bottom-right corner (or choose Edit from an existing card's ⋮ menu or Event Overview screen).
  2. Event Title: Enter a descriptive title (e.g., "Tokyo Trip" or "Launch Day").
  3. Category: Tap a category chip to assign a color and icon to your countdown.
  4. Target Date & Time: Tap the Date card to pick the calendar date, and tap the Time card to select the exact hour and minute.
  5. Time Zone: Tap the Time Zone selector to search and pick any global city or IANA timezone (e.g., America/New_York, Asia/Tokyo, or UTC). Chronos calculates the countdown accurately relative to that timezone.
  6. Notes (Optional): Add reminders, flight numbers, checklists, or event details.
  7. Tap Save Countdown (or Update Countdown) to store the event and schedule its alerts.

3. Alert Notifications

Chronos supports both completion alerts and multiple advance reminders per event.

  1. In the Add/Edit Countdown screen, ensure the Alert Notifications toggle is enabled.
  2. Select one or more preset reminder chips:
    • At event time
    • 15m before
    • 1h before
    • 1d before
    • 1w before
  3. Need a different reminder? Tap + Custom to enter any custom offset in minutes, hours, days, or weeks.
Permission Note: On Android 13 and newer, allow Notifications when prompted so Chronos can deliver your scheduled reminders on time.

4. Managing Categories

The scrollable category bar at the top of the dashboard lets you filter countdowns and see how many events belong to each category.

  • Filter Events: Tap any category pill (or All) to filter your countdown list.
  • Edit Active Category: When a specific category is selected, tap the small pencil icon inside its pill to rename it, change its icon, change its accent color, or delete it.
  • Create a Category: Tap + New Category at the end of the filter bar (or inside the Add/Edit Countdown screen).
  • Manage All Categories: Tap Manage to view all categories in one sheet, see event counts, edit existing categories, or create new ones.

5. Sorting & Reordering Cards

Sorting Options

Tap the Sort button in the top-right corner of the app bar to open the Sort Countdowns sheet and choose from five modes:

Sort Mode Behavior
Chronological (Earliest first) Orders countdowns from soonest / oldest timestamp to latest.
Chronological (Latest first) Orders countdowns from farthest target date to earliest.
Alphabetical (A to Z) Sorts events alphabetically by title (case-insensitive).
Alphabetical (Z to A) Sorts events in reverse alphabetical order by title.
Custom Order Displays cards in your manually arranged drag-and-drop order.

Drag-and-Drop Reordering

  • Grab the drag handle (≡) in the top-right area of any countdown card (by dragging directly or long-pressing) and slide the card up or down.
  • Drag near the top or bottom edge of the list to smoothly auto-scroll through off-screen cards.
  • You can also tap a card's ⋮ menu and choose Move Up or Move Down.
  • Manually moving a card automatically saves the new order and switches the sort mode to Custom Order.

6. Event Overview, Confetti & Sharing

Tapping any countdown card opens the Event Overview screen:

  • Full Countdown Grid: Displays large, easy-to-read cards for Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds.
  • Time Zone Synchronization: Compares the Event Timezone directly against Your Local Time so you never miss a cross-timezone event.
  • Celebrate Button: Triggers a confetti animation and vibration feedback (also triggered automatically when you view an event right as it reaches zero).
  • Share Countdown: Tap Share Countdown to send a formatted snapshot of the remaining time, date, and timezone via any app.

7. Home & Lock Screen Widgets

Chronos provides four home screen widget sizes that update automatically every minute:

  • 2×2 Square: Compact square layout showing the countdown and date.
  • 3×1 Compact: Minimal single-row bar for dense home screens.
  • 4×1 Default: Balanced horizontal countdown card.
  • 5×1 Wide: Expanded wide widget displaying full interval details.

Adding a Widget

  1. Long-press an empty area on your Android home screen and select Widgets.
  2. Find Chronos and drag your preferred widget size onto the home screen.
  3. In the Select Widget Timer screen, pick the countdown you want that widget to track (you can place multiple widgets tracking different events).
  4. Tap any placed widget at any time to open that event's detail screen in Chronos.

8. Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Delayed Notifications or Widgets: Some Android devices apply aggressive battery optimization. If alerts or widgets pause in the background, set Chronos's battery usage to Unrestricted in Android Settings and confirm Notifications and Alarms & Reminders permissions are enabled.
  • Deleting All Data: You can delete individual events anytime, or clear all stored data via Android Settings → Apps → Chronos → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
