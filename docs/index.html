Chronos — Precision Countdown Timer

Track life's milestones, trips, deadlines, and celebrations down to the exact second across years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds — with global timezone synchronization, custom alerts, and home screen widgets.

Read the User Manual Privacy Policy
Private by Design: Chronos works completely offline. No account sign-in, no ads, and no analytics tracking — all of your countdown events, categories, notes, and widget preferences stay locally on your device.

Key Features

6-Unit Live Breakdown

See every event count down second-by-second across Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds. Once an event arrives, Chronos seamlessly tracks elapsed time.

Multi-Timezone Sync

Set each countdown in its destination or event timezone. Inspect both the event's target timezone and your current local device time side-by-side.

Custom Categories & Badges

Filter timers by Celebration, Milestone, Travel, Work, Personal, Fitness, Birthday, Gaming, or create and customize your own categories with unique icons and colors.

Sorting & Drag-and-Drop

Sort chronologically (earliest or latest first), alphabetically (A–Z or Z–A), or long-press and drag any card handle to arrange your custom order.

Customizable Alert Reminders

Receive exact-time notifications when an event arrives plus multiple advance alerts (15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, or any custom offset).

Home & Lock Screen Widgets

Pin countdowns directly to your home screen in four responsive widget layouts: 2×2 Square, 3×1 Compact, 4×1 Default, and 5×1 Wide.

Confetti & One-Tap Sharing

Celebrate milestone arrivals with interactive confetti and haptic feedback, and share formatted countdown summaries to any messaging app.

Offline & Ad-Free

Built natively with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Android Room for fast performance with zero network permissions, zero ads, and zero tracking.
